Know Your Adversary

Why the Florida DOR Is Different from the IRS

Many taxpayers — and even some tax professionals — treat the Florida Department of Revenue as an afterthought compared to the IRS. That's a dangerous mistake. The Florida DOR operates on its own rules, timelines, and enforcement mechanisms that are entirely separate from federal tax law.

No statute of limitations on fraud. If the DOR believes fraud occurred, they can audit you for any year — no expiration.

Personal liability for business owners. Florida DOR can hold you personally liable for your company's unpaid sales tax — even if the business is closed.

Aggressive audit selection. Industries like restaurants, retail, construction, and hospitality are frequent DOR audit targets in South Florida.

License revocation. The DOR can revoke your business license for non-payment, effectively shutting you down.
