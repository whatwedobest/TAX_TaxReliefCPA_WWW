Most Powerful Option

Offer-in-Compromise

An Offer-in-Compromise (OIC) allows qualifying taxpayers to settle their IRS tax debt for less — sometimes significantly less — than the total amount owed. The IRS accepts OICs when it determines that collecting the full liability is either unlikely or would create a financial hardship.

Ronald Muscarella evaluates your eligibility using the same formula the IRS uses and builds the strongest possible offer package. He manages the entire process from submission through negotiation and acceptance.

  • Full financial analysis to determine realistic offer amount
  • Preparation and submission of all IRS forms (656, 433-A/B)
  • Direct negotiation with IRS revenue officers
  • Appeals representation if initial offer is rejected
  • Post-acceptance compliance support
