Understanding Your Situation

What Happens When You Owe the IRS

The IRS collection process follows a predictable path — but its pace can accelerate rapidly, especially if you've been ignoring notices. Understanding where you are in the process is the first step to resolving it.

1
Notice & Demand

The IRS sends CP14 — your first bill. Interest and penalties begin accruing immediately.

2
Escalating Notices

CP501, CP503, CP504 follow — each escalating the urgency and threat level.

3
Final Notice / Intent to Levy

LT11 or Letter 1058 — you have 30 days before levy action begins. This is critical.

4
Levy / Garnishment

The IRS seizes wages, bank accounts, or other assets. Even now, resolution is possible.

At any stage in this process, Tax Relief CPA can intervene. The earlier we get involved, the more options you have — but even if you're at stage 4, we can still help.

Your Options

IRS Resolution Strategies We Use

There's no one-size-fits-all solution to IRS debt. Ronald Muscarella evaluates every available option and recommends the one that produces the best outcome for your specific situation.

Offer-in-Compromise

Settle your total tax debt for a lump sum or short-term payment plan that is less than the full amount owed. Best for taxpayers with limited ability to pay.

Installment Agreement

Pay your balance in monthly installments the IRS agrees to accept. Stops collection action while you pay. Best for taxpayers who can pay over time.

Currently Not Collectible

If you have no ability to pay, the IRS can place your account in "CNC" status — pausing all collection activity until your situation improves.

Penalty Abatement

First-time penalty abatement and reasonable-cause relief can significantly reduce the total balance owed by removing penalties added to your account.

Innocent Spouse Relief

If your spouse (or former spouse) created the tax liability without your knowledge, you may qualify to be relieved of joint tax responsibility.

Collection Due Process Appeals

You have the right to appeal IRS collection actions. Ronald Muscarella files CDP hearings to halt enforcement and negotiate a better resolution outcome.
