About Ronald A. Muscarella, CPA

30+ years of IRS and Florida tax resolution experience — fighting for Fort Lauderdale taxpayers and clients across all of Florida.

A Career Built on Resolving Tax Problems

Ronald Muscarella didn't become a tax resolution CPA by accident. He saw firsthand how devastating IRS enforcement actions could be — and dedicated his career to standing between the IRS and the taxpayers who needed help most.

With over 30 years of experience, Ronald Muscarella has helped hundreds of individuals and businesses navigate some of the most complex IRS and Florida Department of Revenue situations imaginable — from six-figure Offer-in-Compromise settlements to emergency levy releases on the day of seizure.

Located in the Fort Lauderdale / Plantation area, Ronald Muscarella serves clients throughout South Florida and across the entire state. His approach is direct, honest, and results-driven. He won't promise what he can't deliver — but he will fight relentlessly for the outcome you deserve.

"My job is simple: get my clients the best possible resolution and help them stay out of trouble for good. Every client deserves a second chance."
— Ronald A. Muscarella, CPA

Ronald Muscarella's Philosophy

Why Clients Trust Ronald Muscarella

  • CPA Credentials Matter. Ronald Muscarella's CPA license means he has deep accounting knowledge — not just negotiation skills. He can analyze your full financial picture to build the strongest possible case.
  • He Does the Work Himself. We personally handles your case. You won't be handed off to a paralegal or a call center. You work directly with the CPA.
  • No False Promises. Ronald Muscarella tells you exactly what is realistic. If an Offer-in-Compromise isn't right for your situation, he'll tell you — and find the option that is.
